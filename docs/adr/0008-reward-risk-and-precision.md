# Reward Risk and Precision

Meteor Breakers scoring will emphasize risk and precision rather than pure survival, speed, or passive endurance. Hazards grant base points, while a Risk Chain grows through clean shooting and Near-Misses and resets when the Pilot loses a life.

## Consequences

The best play should be brave but controlled. If survival alone dominates scoring, the optimal strategy can become evasive and dull, which conflicts with the intended arcade pressure.
