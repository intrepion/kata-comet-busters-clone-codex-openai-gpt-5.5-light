# Use Three-Tier Fragmentation and Fair Recovery

Comets will fragment through three hazard tiers: Large Comet to Medium Shards to Small Shards to cleared. Pilot collisions will cost one life and respawn the Skimmer near center with a two-second Recovery Window, including protection against immediate spawn overlap.

## Consequences

The Wave loop is readable and deterministic enough to test: each hazard has a known next state, and losing a life should feel punishing but not cascade into unavoidable repeat deaths.
