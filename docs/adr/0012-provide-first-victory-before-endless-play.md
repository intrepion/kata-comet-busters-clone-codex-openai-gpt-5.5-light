# Provide First Victory Before Endless Play

Meteor Breakers will offer a reachable First Victory after clearing Wave 8, then allow Endless Continuation for score chasing. This keeps the arcade lineage while giving both human play and automated verification a concrete success moment.

## Consequences

The Wave 8 Core Comet should feel like a culmination of learned rules, not a genre-breaking boss fight. Endless play can continue beyond that point, but MVP acceptance must not depend on an unbounded run.
